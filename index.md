## Why This Project?

During my clinical rotations, I became increasingly aware of how complex many conditions in gynecology and obstetrics can be, and at the same time, how much potential there is to make medical knowledge more accessible, visual, and interactive.

What fascinates me most is the intersection of **medicine, women’s health, and technology**. How can we present complex pathophysiological processes in a way that allows people not only to memorize them, but to truly understand them?

This question became the starting point for my first independent project.

I chose to focus on **preeclampsia**, a condition in which changes during pregnancy interact across multiple levels, making its underlying pathophysiology difficult to grasp at first glance.

Rather than presenting these mechanisms solely through text, I wanted to make them **visible and interactive**. The learning module therefore guides users step by step from the physiological changes of pregnancy to the pathophysiological processes underlying preeclampsia, placing the two side by side for comparison.

My aim is not to replace a textbook. Instead, I want to explore how digital and interactive formats can help make complex medical concepts more intuitive to understand.

### The Idea Behind It

For me, this project also marks a first step into a field I hope to continue exploring: **How can medicine, design, and technology come together to improve the way medical knowledge is understood and ultimately, the way care is delivered?**

I am still at the very beginning. But that is precisely why I did not want to simply think about these ideas. I wanted to start bringing them to life myself.

This preeclampsia learning experience is my first attempt.
